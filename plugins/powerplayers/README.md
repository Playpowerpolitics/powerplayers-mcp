# Power Players for Cursor

Two remote MCP servers (Streamable HTTP):

- `powerplayers` — https://powerplayers.app/api/mcp/v1 — public, read-only, no sign-in. Search US officials and candidates, citizen ratings with sample counts and dates, rating trends, comparisons, voter-registration links, quote, embed and share helpers.
- `powerplayers-account` — https://powerplayers.app/api/mcp/account — optional, OAuth 2.1 sign-in with your Power Players account. Rate people, keep your ballot, email officials and prepare donation details. Every write shows a preview and waits for your confirmation.

Citizen ratings are voluntary, crowdsourced opinions, not polls. Docs: https://powerplayers.app/connect-ai · Privacy: https://powerplayers.app/privacy
